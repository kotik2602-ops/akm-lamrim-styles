Why we are doing this

So that the teachings happen for everyone

On December 19–21, Peter and Maria Mörtl open a new major cycle of Lamrim teachings in Vienna. The teachings take place live and online.

Organising teachings like these means renting a hall, setting up an online broadcast and many other tasks the team takes on so that the teachings go as well as possible for everyone.

We want the teachings to remain accessible to everyone who seeks the wisdom of Lamrim. So instead of raising the participation fee, we invite everyone who holds this path dear to support the event and become its co-creators.

  • All funds raised go to holding Lamrim 1 in Vienna on December 19–21.
  • Every donation helps the Lamrim teachings continue and reach even more people.

10% of the fundraiser

The red envelope — our gratitude

By ancient tradition, students express gratitude for the teachings with an offering to the Teacher. 10% of everything we raise together will go into a red envelope for Peter and Maria Mörtl.

For us, the Teacher is the highest karmic object. Even a small contribution to this offering plants incredible seeds of abundance in the mind — and is the cause for our Teachers to stay with us, with their wisdom always close.

By taking part in the fundraiser, you become part of this shared offering.

Any questions?

Questions and answers

Where will the money go?

To holding Lamrim 1 in Vienna on December 19–21. 10% of the total will go into the red envelope — an offering to the Teachers in gratitude for the teachings.

How can I improve my chances?

Each contribution to a lot is one ticket. For example, three €5 contributions to the soil from Dragonshead give you three tickets. You can enter several lots.

When and how will the draw take place?

Live at the teachings in Vienna, December 19–21. We’ll announce the winners on stage and then post them in our channels.

Do I have to attend the teachings?

No. You can take part from any country and online. If you win, we’ll contact you and arrange how to get the lot to you.

What if we don’t reach the €5,000 goal?

All six lots will still be drawn, and the funds will go to the teachings. The grand prize — a ticket to the next Lamrim — is drawn only if we reach the goal.

Can I support without entering the draw?

Of course. Use the “Donate” button.
