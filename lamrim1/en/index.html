What is Lamrim

A complete map of the path to enlightenment

“Lamrim” means “stages of the path”. It is a step-by-step system that shows:

  • 01where inner transformation begins
  • 02which practices are needed at each stage
  • 03why it matters to take these steps in a particular order
  • 04how to work with your mind in daily life
  • 05how to connect everyday goals with moving toward the highest goal

We may know many separate practices, listen to all kinds of teachings and understand complex ideas. But without a clear map it can be hard to see where we are right now and what our next step should be.

Lamrim helps bring this knowledge together into one path.

It can support change in particular areas of life: relationships, health, fulfillment and well-being. But its true depth is far greater: it is a path of gradually transforming the whole person.

The first stage

A big beginning

Geshe Michael taught this Lamrim cycle over many years. Peter and Maria Moertl want to preserve this knowledge, systematize it and gradually shape it into a sequence of courses.

December 19–21 is a complete first stage in its own right, after which you will:

  • 1understand the role of shamatha on the spiritual path
  • 2gain knowledge about developing concentration
  • 3learn about the obstacles in meditation and how to work with them
  • 4see the connection between meditation, wisdom and emptiness
  • 5receive practices you can bring into your daily life

Steps on the path

From knowledge to experience

Knowledge changes lives when it becomes practice

You can hear many beautiful teachings and even understand them well. But real change begins when that knowledge enters our daily life. It is regular practice that helps us:

  • notice the habitual reactions of our mind
  • gradually change the causes that create our reality
  • develop stability and clarity
  • carry knowledge from our notes into real relationships, decisions and actions
  • keep our direction when difficulties arise
  • step by step, become the person we want to be

That is why the new cycle is designed as more than a series of lectures. The teachers want to combine knowledge with meditation, discussion, personal practice and, later on, retreats.

Not just knowing the path, but truly walking it.

The December theme · why the cycle begins with meditation

Meditation Pro: shamatha and wisdom

The first stages of the path
we’ll come back here nextShamatha and wisdom
where we begin in December

Lamrim is usually studied starting from the first stages of the path. But Geshe Michael structured his cycle in an unusual way: first he taught shamatha, meditation and wisdom, and only then returned to the other steps.

Peter and Maria Moertl will follow the same route.

Shamatha

Deep stability of mind

You will begin to learn how to develop clear, stable concentration:

  • how the nine levels of concentration work
  • what keeps us from holding our attention
  • which antidotes to apply to the obstacles
  • how to gradually make your meditation deep and stable
Wisdom

Preparing for the direct perception of emptiness

Shamatha is not simply the ability to sit for a long time with your eyes closed or to calm your thoughts.

Deep, single-pointed concentration is needed to prepare the mind for the direct perception of emptiness, an experience that can completely change how we understand ourselves and the world.

No one can walk this whole path in three days. But in December you will receive the map, the knowledge and the practical tools to begin.

A time of magic and change

December is a special time

A time when we take stock, look back on the past year and feel especially clearly what we want to change in the next one.

Three days with Peter and Maria Moertl are a chance to step out of your usual routine, immerse yourself fully in practice and look at your life through the lens of the seed system teachings.

Not in snatches between work and daily chores, but by dedicating three full days to it, together with the teachers and fellow practitioners.

It is a chance to plant new seeds for the changes you want to see in your life in the new year.

Real change begins when knowledge becomes daily practice.

Who these teachings are for

Lamrim is needed at every stage of the path

01

If you are just starting out

Lamrim can be a wonderful doorway into Tibetan wisdom.

You don’t need to know any special terms in advance or to have taken ACI courses. You will receive a clear map: how to work with yourself, which steps to take and how to gradually change your life.

Many of Geshe Michael’s students first discovered the Dharma precisely through Lamrim.

02

If you are studying or have completed the courses of ACI

ACI courses explore individual topics of Tibetan philosophy in depth. Lamrim helps you see how all this knowledge fits together and in what sequence to apply it on the path.

Some topics will be familiar, but much will open up in more detail and from a different angle.

03

If you have received the direct Diamond Way transmission

Receiving the transmission is not the end of your training; it is the opening of a door.

In Peter Moertl’s words, practicing the Diamond Way without a solid foundation in Lamrim is extremely difficult. It is Lamrim that helps root the knowledge in your mind, keep you heading in the right direction and turn deep practices into living experience rather than just beautiful words.

Peter Moertl himself studied Lamrim with Geshe Michael alongside his practice of the Diamond Way.

Lamrim and the Diamond Way do not contradict each other: they support and nourish one another.

The power of community

Why being together matters

A community gives you invisible strength and support on the path

When you are surrounded by people who share your values and strive for similar goals, it becomes easier to move forward.

A community helps you discover and strengthen your best qualities, stay inspired, keep your direction through difficult times and continue practicing when your own strength is not enough.

Today it is especially important not to be left alone with your spiritual path.

In-person gatherings help you step out of your usual routine, spend several days close to the teachers and feel the support of people moving in the same direction.

It is a space where knowledge becomes living experience:

  • through direct contact with the teachers
  • shared meditations and practices
  • questions and discussions
  • wisdom groups
  • morning yoga
  • connecting with like-minded people

If you can’t make it to Vienna, you can join online for free. But if you are able to come, we warmly recommend spending these three days together with the teachers and the sangha.

Still have doubts?

Questions and answers

Wasn’t there already Lamrim in April?

In April we studied one specific practice: a meditation called “Lamrim”, that is, a meditation on the stages of the path.

In December, a new systematic cycle of teachings begins on Lamrim as a complete map of the path. It is not a repeat of the April program, but the next and much larger stage.

Those who attended in April will be able to continue and deepen their practice. Newcomers can join without any prior preparation.

Why do I need Lamrim if I have already received the direct Diamond Way transmission?

The transmission opens the door, but it does not replace further study and daily practice.

Lamrim gives you the foundation and the step-by-step map you need to understand and practice the Diamond Way. In Peter Moertl’s experience, the two support and strengthen each other.

Peter Moertl himself studied Lamrim with Geshe Michael alongside the Diamond Way.

I’ve taken ACI 3. Will this be a repeat?

Some basic topics may overlap, and that is natural. But the goal and depth of the program are different.

ACI 3 gives a general introduction to meditation: how to start practicing and working with your mind.

The new cycle focuses on developing shamatha and preparing the mind for the direct perception of emptiness. So the December teachings will not be a repeat, but a continuation and deepening of your practice.

I’m a beginner. Is this right for me?

Yes. Lamrim lays out the path step by step, so it can be a wonderful starting point even for someone who isn’t yet familiar with ACI courses or Tibetan philosophy.

The teachers will explain the material in an accessible way, introducing the key concepts gradually.

Can I join if I wasn’t at the April teachings?

Yes. The April program is not a requirement for taking part.

The new cycle begins in December, so you can join without any prior preparation.

Will we cover all of Lamrim in three days?

No. December marks the first stage of a large cycle.

At the same time, the gathering has value in its own right: you will receive concrete knowledge and practices that you can apply right after the event.

We will announce the format and dates of the next stages separately.

Do I have to take part in the entire cycle that follows?

No. You don’t need to make a decision for several years ahead right now.

After the December gathering, you can decide for yourself whether you want to continue.

Can I take part online?

Yes. The online stream will be free.

If you are able to come to Vienna, we recommend attending in person: live contact with the teachers, practicing together and the shared space of the community create a completely different level of immersion.
