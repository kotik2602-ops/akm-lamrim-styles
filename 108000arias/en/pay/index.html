Payment not going through?

Most often it's a card setting. Go through these steps — usually that's enough.

  1. 1

    Check that there are funds on your card.

  2. 2

    Check that online payments are allowed and the limit is high enough.

  3. 3

    After checking, try again.

  4. 4

    If the payment still fails, ask your bank why it didn't go through.

  5. 5

    If the bank says the problem is on the payment service's side, message us.
