Our mission

What is the “108,000 Arias” project

An Arya is someone who has seen emptiness directly. Our goal is for 108,000 people to begin studying ACI courses and walk this path.

The goal of this study and practice is to see directly how reality exists. After that, a person becomes completely different — with a new level of awareness, strength and possibilities.

We want as many people as possible to learn about ACI courses, complete all the open courses and move on to the secret teachings.

To plant the seeds for completing the courses successfully and reaching the main goal — seeing emptiness directly — the best thing you can do is help others with the same goals.

Reports and questions

Frequently asked questions

We publish monthly reports on the funds raised in the project's channel.

Where exactly does the money go?

Donations go into the common budget of the Classical Wisdom Academy. The main areas of spending in 2026 are listed in “Where your donations go”.

Why monthly rather than one-time?

Regular support lets the Academy plan its work a year ahead. And in terms of seeds, regularity makes them as powerful and viable as possible. A one-time gift is also very valuable — every amount matters.

How do I get access to the Meditation Club?

Everyone who sets up a monthly subscription from €5 gets access to the Classical Wisdom Academy's Meditation Club.

My payment doesn't go through — what should I do?

Check that there are funds on your card and that online payments are allowed. If your bank says the problem is on the payment service's side, contact support: @Ivanlord. You can also manage or cancel a recurring payment on the payment page.

Where can I see the reports?

Every month in the “108,000 Arias” channel we publish the amount raised over the previous month and thank every donor.
