Who is Peter Moertl

Peter Moertl, originally from Austria, brings together two worlds: business and Tibetan Buddhist philosophy. He holds an MBA from the Vienna University of Economics and Business and spent more than 20 years in major international companies in Austria, Germany and Sweden, known for his communication skills and his talent for building strong partnerships.

After personal tragedies in 2007, his search for answers about life and death led him to Geshe Michael Roach’s book “The Diamond Cutter”. It sparked a deep interest in Tibetan Buddhism: Peter began studying the 18 courses of the Asian Classics Institute (ACI) and meditating daily. In 2009 he volunteered at the Diamond Mountain Retreat Center in Arizona, helping prepare a three-year retreat while continuing his ACI studies.

By 2014 Peter had completed all 18 ACI courses, and he keeps learning within this lineage. Together with his wife Maria Moertl he translates ACI course materials into Russian, so that this precious knowledge stays accessible — and the transmission of Tibetan wisdom continues in the Western world.

The biggest change ACI brought into Peter’s life: far less anxiety — through understanding the real causes behind people and events.

For Peter, intellectual understanding is the first step; the next is putting the karmic principles into practice to make your wishes come true. His own biggest “success story” is the conscious creation of his partnership with Maria Moertl.
